GREENBOOK ATTRIBUTION & LICENSES

Attribution

GreenBook is built on open data. Everything under /data/open/ is a collective database licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0 — the full text ships with the site at /data/open/LICENSE, and per-source credits at /data/open/ATTRIBUTION.md. Share-alike applies to that tree.

Course registry

OpenGolfAPI — © OpenGolfAPI contributors, ODbL. Identity, location, pars, stroke index, yardages, and per-tee ratings where crawled. OpenGolfAPI itself contains information from OpenStreetMap. Registry fields are cited as OPENGOLFAPI — not USGA — until independently verified per course.

Hole geometry & hazards

OpenStreetMap — © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Hole centerlines, bunker / water / green polygons, course boundaries, retrieved via the Overpass API.

Elevation

USGS 3DEP — public domain (U.S. Government work). 1/3 arc-second GeoTIFF tiles via the AWS Open Data program, sampled along hole centerlines; EPQS point API as fallback.

Weather

NOAA / National Weather Service — public domain. Precipitation observations fetched client-side from api.weather.gov at page load; not stored in the open tree.

The scoring layer

Axis outputs, verdict states, heuristic parameters, scoring configuration, and editorial overlays under /data/starpoint/ are proprietary — © Starpoint LLC, all rights reserved. The scoring layer joins the open tree at render time, by slug, and never writes into it. Computed scores are never part of the ODbL collective database.

Corrections

Conflicting sources are flagged, never averaged. If you have the authoritative document for a flagged field (association handicap table, published card), open an issue at github.com/CaptainMig/greenbook.
